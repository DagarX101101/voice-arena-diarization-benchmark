📐 How the Custom Score is Calculated

Standard DER penalizes Missed Speech, False Alarms, and Speaker Confusion identically. In commercial audio systems, costs diverge: speaker confusion scrambles agent QA in call centres, while missed speech drops words in speech-to-text.

Custom Score = 100.0 × max(0.0, 1.0 − Category-Weighted Error)
