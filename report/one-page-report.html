Evaluation Framework & Custom Scoring
  • Why Category-Specific Scores: Generic DER treats Miss, False Alarm, and Confusion identically. In production, error costs diverge drastically. Custom Score = $100 \times \max(0, 1 - \text{Category-Weighted Error})$.
  • Call Centre Telephony ($w_{\text{conf}}=0.35$): High confusion distorts compliance audits and agent QA scorecards; missed turns lose legal disclosures.
  • Live Transcription ($w_{\text{miss}}=0.50$): Missed speech directly drops words in downstream ASR pipelines.
  • Office Meetings ($w_{\text{conf}}=0.40$): Speaker confusion scrambles action-item assignments in automated minutes.
Key Empirical Findings (Models 1–4)
  • Streaming Offline Parity: Model 4 (Streaming Sortformer) matches offline accuracy (99.83 vs 99.75 in meetings; 93.07 vs 93.11 in live audio) on CPU (~7.65s runtime).
  • Clustering Dominates Telephony: Model 3 (NeMo Clustering) achieves 72.18% DER / 2.02% confusion on calls vs pyannote's 97.14% DER / 27.81% confusion.
  • VAD Aggressiveness Trade-off: Model 1 (pyannote) achieves minimal call miss (6.30%), but triggers severe false alarms (63.04% on calls, 13.19% on live audio).

Strategic Production Deployment Takeaways
  • Live ASR & Captioning: Deploy NVIDIA NeMo Streaming Sortformer for immediate real-time speaker turns with zero session re-clustering.
  • Contact Center QA & Compliance: Deploy NeMo Clustering Diarizer or BUT-FIT VBx for robust speaker attribution across 8 kHz telephony channels.
  • Multi-Party Overlap: Deploy NeMo Sortformer (Offline) or ESPnet EEND-EDA to separate simultaneous speakers.
