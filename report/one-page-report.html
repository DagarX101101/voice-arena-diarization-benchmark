Evaluation Framework & Custom Scoring
  • Why Category-Specific Scores: Generic DER treats Miss, False Alarm, and Confusion identically. In production, error costs diverge drastically. Custom Score = $100 \times \max(0, 1 - \text{Category-Weighted Error})$.
  • Call Centre Telephony ($w_{\text{conf}}=0.35$): High confusion distorts compliance audits and agent QA scorecards; missed turns lose statutory disclosures.
  • Live Transcription ($w_{\text{miss}}=0.50$): Missed speech directly drops words in downstream speech-to-text pipelines.
  • Office Meetings ($w_{\text{conf}}=0.40$): Speaker confusion scrambles action-item assignments in automated minutes.
Key Empirical Findings Across Systems
  • Streaming Offline Parity: Model 4 (Streaming Sortformer) matches offline accuracy (99.83 vs 99.75 in meetings; 93.07 vs 93.11 in live audio) on CPU (~7.65s runtime).
  • Clustering Dominates Telephony: Models 10 & 3 achieve 2.02%–2.10% confusion on 8 kHz calls vs pyannote's 27.81% confusion.
  • VAD Aggressiveness Trade-off: Model 1 (pyannote) achieves minimal call miss (6.30%), but triggers elevated false alarms (63.04% on calls, 13.19% on live audio).

Architectural Paradigms Evaluated
  • Self-Attention Sortformers (Models 2, 4): Direct transformer-based speaker sorting resolving multi-speaker turns without heuristic clustering.
  • Modular Neural Clustering (Models 1, 3, 5, 6, 8): Multi-stage pipelines separating acoustic VAD, deep embedding extraction, and graph spectral/AHC clustering.
  • End-to-End Multi-Speaker (Model 7): Joint multi-label neural networks predicting overlapping speaker tracks simultaneously.
  • Probabilistic State Models (Models 9, 10): Hidden Markov Models with Variational Bayes or recurrent state transitions.
Strategic Production Deployment Takeaways
  • Live ASR & Captioning: Deploy NVIDIA NeMo Streaming Sortformer for immediate real-time speaker turns with zero session re-clustering.
  • Contact Center QA & Compliance: Deploy NeMo Clustering Diarizer or BUT-FIT VBx for robust speaker attribution across 8 kHz telephony channels.
  • Multi-Party Overlap: Deploy NeMo Sortformer (Offline) or ESPnet EEND-EDA to separate simultaneous speakers.
