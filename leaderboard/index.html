Custom Score Definition

Custom Score = 100.0 × max(0.0, 1.0 − Category-Weighted Error)

Higher is better. Scores range from 0.00 to 100.00. 100 represents zero missed speech, zero false alarms, and zero speaker confusion.

Category-Weighted Error (CWE)

CWE = (w_miss · T_miss + w_fa · T_fa + w_conf · T_conf) / T_ref

Standard DER applies equal cost to all errors. The Voice Arena Benchmark penalizes errors according to their actual business consequences.
